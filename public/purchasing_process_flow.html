Cash Advance to Liquidation

Cash advance and liquidation

Cash advances are limited to ₱10,000. The requester submits the liquidation on the same cash advance record.

  1. RequesterSubmits the request

    Enter the amount and submit the cash advance. The request goes to the Department Head.

  2. Department HeadApproves the request

    Review and approve the cash advance. The status becomes For Release.

  3. Accounting ManagerReleases the cash advance. Status moves to For Liquidation.

    Release the cash to the requester. The same record moves to For Liquidation.

  4. Original requesterSubmits actual amount and supporting attachment

    Open the released cash advance, enter the actual amount, and attach supporting documents. Submit it for Department Head approval.

  5. Department HeadApproves the liquidation

    Review the actual amount and supporting documents. Approval sends the record to Accounting for SAP entry.

  6. AccountingEnters the SAP document number

    Save the SAP document number to move the record to For Accounting Completion.

  7. Accounting ManagerMarks the liquidation Completed

    Review the liquidation and mark the cash advance record Completed.

Request for Payment

Direct payment RFP

For payments that do not require liquidation. GM-stage approval applies at every amount.

  1. RequesterSubmits the request

    Submit the payee, amount, and budget allocation with Requires liquidation set to No.

  2. Department HeadApproves the request

    Review the RFP and send it to For GM Approval.

  3. Assigned approverApproves at the GM stage

    The assigned approver signs at any amount. The RFP moves to For Release.

  4. For ReleaseRFP is ready for Accounting

    Accounting can now process payment and enter the SAP document number.

  5. AccountingEnters SAP document number and marks the RFP Completed

    Save the SAP document number at release. The RFP moves to Completed.

RFP requiring liquidation

Follow these steps to complete the RFP, then file the linked liquidation below.

  1. RequesterSubmits the request

    Submit the payee, amount, and budget allocation with Requires liquidation set to Yes.

  2. Department HeadApproves the request

    Review the RFP and send it to For GM Approval.

  3. Assigned approverApproves at the GM stage

    The assigned approver signs at any amount. The RFP moves to For Release.

  4. For ReleaseRFP is ready for Accounting

    Accounting can now process payment and enter the SAP document number.

  5. AccountingEnters SAP document number and marks the RFP Completed

    Save the SAP document number at release. The completed RFP becomes eligible for linked liquidation.

Liquidation linked to a completed RFP

The RFP must be marked Requires liquidation. The linked liquidation goes through its own GM-stage approval at every amount.

  1. Original requesterCreates linked liquidation with actual amount and supporting attachment

    Open the completed RFP and choose Create Linked Liquidation. Enter the actual amount and attach supporting documents.

  2. Department HeadApproves the request

    Review and approve the linked liquidation so Accounting can enter its SAP number.

  3. AccountingEnters the SAP document number

    Save the SAP document number for the liquidation. It moves to For GM Approval at any amount.

  4. Assigned approverApproves at the GM stage

    Approve the linked liquidation separately from the RFP. It moves to For Accounting Completion.

  5. Accounting ManagerMarks the liquidation Completed

    Review the linked liquidation and mark it Completed.

Standalone Liquidation

₱10,000 or below

After SAP entry, the request moves directly to Accounting completion.

  1. RequesterSubmits the request

    Enter the expense amount and attach supporting documents. Submit for Department Head approval.

  2. Department HeadApproves the request

    Review the expense and approve it for SAP entry.

  3. AccountingEnters the SAP document number

    Save the SAP document number. At ₱10,000 or below, a standalone liquidation moves directly to For Accounting Completion.

  4. Accounting ManagerMarks the liquidation Completed

    Review the liquidation and mark it Completed.

Above ₱10,000

The assigned approver reviews the liquidation after SAP entry.

  1. RequesterSubmits the request

    Enter the expense amount and attach supporting documents. Submit for Department Head approval.

  2. Department HeadApproves the request

    Review the expense and approve it for SAP entry.

  3. AccountingEnters the SAP document number

    Save the SAP document number. Above ₱10,000, a standalone liquidation moves to For GM Approval.

  4. Assigned approverApproves at the GM stage

    Approve the liquidation so it moves to For Accounting Completion.

  5. Accounting ManagerMarks the liquidation Completed

    Review the liquidation and mark it Completed.
